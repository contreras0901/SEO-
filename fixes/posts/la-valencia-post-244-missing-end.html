Music, and the moment that mattered

Two sources of music, on purpose. Paper Moon Music played classical guitar for the ceremony and cocktail hour, and DJ Sammy Denatto of Dancing DJ Productions took the reception, so the day moved from strings by the sea to a dance floor without the room ever going quiet. The guitarists’ corner was set the way the altar was: two chairs, two guitars, and around them the same blue-and-yellow Spanish pots, ferns, and lanterns from the ceremony, carried inside so the music looked like it belonged to the garden. That is the small decision guests do not notice and photographers always do.
