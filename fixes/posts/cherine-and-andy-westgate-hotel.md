### A Black-Tie Persian Wedding at The Westgate Hotel

If you consider yourself a black-tie Parisian aficionado, this one is for you. The Westgate Hotel already carries a magical Parisian setting; it is one of the few downtown San Diego properties where European formality is the native language of the room rather than something you have to build. Cherine and Andy's wedding leaned into that entirely, and Bella Mia was brought in to design the florals and supply the rentals that carried the look from the Sofreh to the last table.
