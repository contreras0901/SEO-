Good questions

Before you ask

How long does a full launch take?

Six to eight weeks for the complete package — formation filed in the first three weeks, website designed and built by week six, and SEO and marketing live by week eight. Website-only projects typically run four to six weeks.

Do I need an LLC before we start on the website?

No — that's the point of doing both under one roof. We start the formation paperwork and the design at the same time, so neither waits on the other. If you already have your entity, we simply begin at the website.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Every build ends with a handoff session where we walk you through editing your own text, photos, and prices — and you'll have a recording to refer back to. You own everything: the domain, the site, and the accounts.

What does SEO actually include?

On-page SEO (titles, descriptions, structure, and speed), local SEO (your Google Business Profile, service-area pages, and citations), and a simple content plan so you keep ranking after launch. We focus on being found by nearby clients ready to buy — not vanity traffic.

Do you work with clients outside your area?

Yes — we're based in San Diego and work with founders in all 50 states. Consultations, design reviews, and training happen over video, entity filings are handled in your state, and your SEO is tuned to wherever your clients are: your city, your region, or the whole country.

What is AI/LLM SEO, and do I need it?

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation, those tools answer from websites they can read and trust. AI/LLM SEO structures your site so it gets cited in those answers — entity markup, answer-ready content, and consistent business details across the web. If your clients ask AI for recommendations (and more do every month), it's the newest way to be found first.

How is pricing structured?

Each package is quoted as a single flat scope after a complimentary consultation, so you know the full investment before anything begins. State filing fees are passed through at cost — we never mark up government fees.
