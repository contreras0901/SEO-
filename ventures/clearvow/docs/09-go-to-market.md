# Deliverable 9: Go-to-market and growth strategy

## 1. The two-sided sequencing problem, solved by order of operations

Supply first, but only the supply that already trusts the founder. Demand second, through search queries that incumbents do not answer (price queries). Content third, because it compounds both.

Weeks 1 to 4 (pre-launch): 150 claimed San Diego vendor profiles with published prices. Source: Bella Mia's past vendor teams, Ethereal's planner and venue contacts, the Concierge hotel relationships (Loews Coronado, Hotel del Coronado, Park Hyatt Aviara, Westgate, La Valencia), and the vendors already credited in the Bella Mia real-wedding posts in this repository. Vendors are told exactly what the deal is: free listing, prices required, inquiries verified.

Weeks 5 to 12 (launch): 25 real weddings and 14 price guides live; paid search test; vendor community posts; first 20 paying vendors.

## 3. Funnel design and KPIs

| Stage | Couple | KPI and target (month 6, San Diego) | Vendor | KPI and target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Organic sessions on price guides and category pages | 8,000 monthly organic sessions | Credited in a real wedding; community mention | 400 claim-page visits/month |
| Acquisition | Account created | 8% of sessions | Profile claimed or created | 35% of claim-page visits |
| Activation | First verified inquiry sent | 45% of accounts within 14 days | Profile live with prices and pledge | 60% of claimed within 7 days |
| Revenue | n/a | | First unlock or Pro checkout | 15% of live vendors within 60 days |
| Retention | Returns within 30 days; second inquiry | 50% | Monthly paid churn | Under 5% |
| Referral | Shares vendor team or submits wedding | 10% of married couples | Credits others in a submission | 70% of submissions credit 4+ vendors |

**North-star metric:** verified inquiries that receive a reply within 48 hours. It captures supply quality, demand quality, and the product's promise in one number.

**Cost metrics:** cost per verified inquiry (target under $25 blended, under $18 by month 12); vendor CAC (target under $200 blended).

## 4. Messaging by audience

- **Couples:** "See the price before you ask." Secondary: "Verified inquiries get answered." Proof: live reply-time stats on every profile.
- **Vendors:** "Inquiries, not leads." Secondary: "No contracts. Ranking you cannot buy." Proof: public ranking rules, refund policy, no sales call.
- **Venues:** "Every wedding at your venue, credited and ranked." Secondary: "Your price guide page, built from real data." Proof: venue price-guide pages.

Legal note for marketing: competitors are never named in a defamatory way. "Pay-to-play" is a category description, not an accusation against a company.

## 5. Launch sequence (San Diego)

1. Private beta: 30 vendors from the founder's network claim and publish prices; 10 real weddings published; price guides generated.
2. Soft launch: category pages indexed; paid search test starts; vendor community posts with the ranking rules as the hook.
3. Public launch: press angle for local media and wedding trade (the FTC story makes "the marketplace that publishes prices" newsworthy); founding-vendor rate closes at 50.
4. Month 4 to 6: Spotlight opens to Preferred; venue partnership pilots with two hotels; the first San Diego price report published as PR.

## 6. Metro expansion playbook (for Deliverable 10)

A metro opens when: a local launch partner (planner or venue) is signed; 80 vendors have pre-claimed with prices; 10 real weddings are ready; 14 price guides can be generated. Budget per metro: $15k to $25k over 90 days. Do not open a metro without a launch partner.
