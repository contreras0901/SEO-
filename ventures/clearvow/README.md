## The MVP

`app/` is a working Next.js 15 application: public marketplace with published prices and programmatic SEO pages, couple accounts with verified structured inquiries and a planning dashboard, vendor accounts with pricing, pledge, inquiry inbox, real-wedding submissions, analytics and Stripe billing, and an admin review console. It builds clean, lints clean, type-checks clean, and passes a 15-step end-to-end test through the real UI. See `app/README.md`.

## Evidence labels

VERIFIED (read on the live site or a primary source this week), REPORTED (third party, often a competitor), INFERENCE (our reasoning), ASSUMPTION (a number chosen for the model). Loverly's and The Knot's main pages blocked automated fetching; Loverly's vendor pricing page was read in full, and The Knot was analyzed from its own press releases and third-party reporting.
