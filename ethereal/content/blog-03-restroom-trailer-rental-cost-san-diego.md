## How we compare to a porta potty

A standard portable toilet costs a fraction of a trailer. It also has no flushing toilet, no running water, no climate control, and no lighting. For a job site with a small crew, that tradeoff usually favors the porta potty. For a wedding, a gala, or a brand activation, it usually does not. We lay out the full comparison in [restroom trailer vs. porta potty](/blog/restroom-trailer-vs-porta-potty) and on [why a trailer](/why-a-trailer).

## How to get an exact number

The published rates get you most of the way. For an exact quote we need:

1. Event date, or project start and end dates.
2. Address of the site.
3. Guest count or crew size.
4. Event length, including setup and teardown.
5. Which size and design line you are leaning toward, if you know.
6. Whether the site has power and water near the placement spot.
7. Whether you need an accessible stall.
