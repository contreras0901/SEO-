# Wedding Restroom Trailer Checklist: Questions to Ask Your Venue

A restroom trailer at a wedding is simple on the day if the details were handled weeks before. Most problems we see come from one thing: nobody asked the venue the right questions early. This checklist is built so you, your planner, or your venue coordinator can go through it once and hand us the answers.

Each section ends with what we need to hear back from you.

## Before you start: do you need a trailer at all?

Ask the venue how many restrooms guests can use and whether they can handle your guest count for the full event. Many estate and backyard venues have two or three bathrooms inside a house. That is not enough for a long reception, and most couples do not want guests inside the house anyway. If the venue has permanent restrooms near the reception area, you may not need a trailer. If not, keep going. See [why a trailer](/why-a-trailer) and [wedding restrooms](/wedding-restrooms).

## 1. Access: can the trailer get in?

The trailer arrives on a tow vehicle. It needs a route in, a place to turn, and a place to sit.

Ask the venue:

- What is the width of the entrance gate or driveway?
- Are there low branches, arches, or overhead wires on the route in?
- Is the driveway paved, gravel, dirt, or grass?
- Is there a slope on the route or at the placement spot?
- Is there room to turn around, or will the driver need to back in?

**What we need from you:** gate width, surface type, and a photo of the route in if possible.

## 2. Placement: where will it sit?

The trailer should be close enough that guests use it without a long walk, and far enough that it is not in the photos.

Ask the venue:

- Where have past couples placed a restroom trailer?
- Is there a level spot within [FOUNDER: distance] of the reception area?
- Can the trailer be screened by landscaping, a hedge, or a fence?
- Will the spot be lit at night, or does it need path lighting?

[FOUNDER: your guidance on ideal placement distance and any limits on positioning.]

**What we need from you:** the proposed spot, marked on a map or described in relation to the reception area.

## 3. Power: what will run it?

The trailer's lighting and climate control need electricity.

Ask the venue:

- Is there an exterior outlet within reach of the placement spot?
- What type of outlet is it?
- Is that circuit shared with the band, the caterer, or landscape lighting?
- Can a generator be used on the property if needed?

[FOUNDER: exact power requirements, outlet type, cord-run limit, and whether you supply a generator.]

**What we need from you:** outlet location and type, or confirmation that a generator is needed.

## 4. Water: where does it come from?

The trailer has flushing toilets and running water at the sinks.

Ask the venue:

- Is there a hose spigot within reach of the placement spot?
- Are there restrictions on water use on the property?

[FOUNDER: whether the trailer carries its own fresh water, whether a hose connection is required, and what happens to waste water.]

**What we need from you:** spigot location, or confirmation that none is available.

## 5. Permits and approvals: who needs to say yes?

Most private property placements do not need a permit. Some do, and some venues have their own rules.

Ask the venue:

- Does the venue require approval of outside restroom vendors?
- Does the venue require a certificate of insurance from the vendor?
- Does the property sit in an HOA that restricts trailers or deliveries?
- If the trailer must sit on a public street, does the city require a permit?

[FOUNDER: whether you can provide a certificate of insurance, and whether you have ever needed a permit for a wedding placement.]

**What we need from you:** the venue's vendor requirements and any HOA rules.

## 6. Timing: when can it arrive and leave?

Ask the venue:

- What is the earliest a vendor can arrive on the wedding day?
- Can the trailer be delivered the day before?
- What is the latest a vendor can be on site after the event?
- Can the trailer stay overnight and be picked up the next morning?

[FOUNDER: your standard delivery window, whether day-before delivery is available, and your pickup policy for evening weddings.]

**What we need from you:** the venue's vendor arrival window and the end time of your event.
