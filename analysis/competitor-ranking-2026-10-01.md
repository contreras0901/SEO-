### Reading the table

- **Willmus wins on every axis** and on proof by a mile: 400+ reviews across two platforms, five straight Knot awards. Nobody in the set is close. Not a target to chase head-on.
- **Wedding Agency San Diego is the instructive one.** Founded 2023, fewer reviews than Creative Affairs or Sisti, and still second, because its architecture is exactly right: one page per city with hotel names, one page per package with prices, a vendor directory, a blog. It appeared for four of six queries. That is what good structure does for a young domain.
- **Sisti & Co is Bella Mia's closest positioning rival**: planner plus in-house florals, venue posts, Knot Hall of Fame. It is the benchmark for the floral-and-styling angle.
- **Serene ranks 8th here, not 1st.** Its content is the best in the set, but its core pages are weak and its own site appeared for none of the six queries. Bella Mia has been measuring itself against Serene's blog, which is the right thing to copy, and against the wrong overall target.
- **Bella Mia's technical score (7) is now above Serene, First Comes Love, and Kristine Smith.** That is the 29 Sep work. The remaining 3 points: the `/home` duplicate of `/`, unrenamed image files, and no portfolio page (it redirects to the blog).
