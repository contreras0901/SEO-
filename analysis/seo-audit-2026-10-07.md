## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

Every indexable page now has a unique title, a meta description (except the two category archives, which need a WordPress setting only the Founder can reach), exactly one H1, and the primary phrase in the title and H1 where it belongs. Four of the six real-wedding posts had **no H1 at all** until today; that is fixed. Three thin or broken URLs that Google could index were redirected. What the site still lacks is not on-page hygiene: it is **proof and presence**. Bella Mia's own pages appeared for **none** of the seven non-branded queries observed today, and the branded query surfaced only the WeddingWire listing. On the 60-point rubric used since 1 October, Bella Mia moves from 29 to **32**, still 10th of 11 but now one point behind First Comes Love and ahead of Kristine Smith; Serene Events (34) is within reach after the next two or three posts. Reaching the middle of the table is a content-and-reviews job over months, not a settings job.
