## 3. What Serene Events & Design is doing differently (the aggressive read)

Everything below is OBSERVED from indexed URLs/titles/snippets unless labeled INFERENCE.

1. **One post per venue, titled with the venue.** Observed real-wedding posts: Scripps Seaside Forum (La Jolla), Estancia La Jolla, Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines, Tivoli Italian Villa (Fallbrook), Botanica (Oceanside), Rancho Bernardo Inn, Park Hyatt Aviara, Fairmont Grand Del Mar, Rancho Valencia (Rancho Santa Fe), Twin Oaks Golf Course (San Marcos), Vista Valley Country Club, Ultimate Skybox, Carmel Mountain Ranch Estate, The Westgate, La Venta Inn, George Peabody Library (Baltimore). INFERENCE: each is a landing page for "[venue] wedding" queries and a proof page for couples already booked there. That is the highest-intent traffic a planner can get: the couple has a venue, a date and a budget.
2. **Venue-guide content that precedes the planner decision.** "Best San Diego Wedding Venues" (Apr 2022), "San Diego European Inspired Venues" (Mar 2024). These capture couples earlier in the journey.
3. **Planning-tip content.** "11 Ways To Elevate Your Wedding" (2021), "How To Create A Modern Elegant Wedding" (2023), "How To Leave a Wedding Vendor Review" (2022). Light, but it keeps the blog alive and gives internal links.
4. **Sustained cadence.** Dated posts from June 2021 through April 2025. INFERENCE: roughly 4–6 posts a year, which is enough for a small planner.
5. **Venue/city taxonomy.** Tag archives exist for la-jolla-wedding, torrey-pines-wedding, scripps-wedding, scripps-seaside-forum-wedding, tivoli-wedding, botanica-wedding-venue, san-diego-wedding-venue, trademark-venues, la-venta-inn, baltimore-wedding. INFERENCE: this happens automatically on WordPress (URL pattern /YYYY/MM/DD/slug/ + /tag/ + /category/). It also creates thin duplicate pages, see weaknesses.
6. **Press and award signals surfaced publicly.** "Featured in SMP, Magnolia Rouge, Ruffled, Southern California Bride and The White Wren"; Knot "Best of Weddings" 4× (THIRD-PARTY CLAIMS from site/listing snippets).
7. **Instagram profile name carries the keyword.** Profile title observed as "San Diego Wedding Planner (@sereneeventsanddesign)". The brand's social profile itself ranks for a service+city string.
8. **Consistent home city.** Encinitas on every directory observed. One city, one story.
9. **Named team with roles.** Korina (lead planner/owner), Giselle and Eileen (lead coordinators for Wedding Management). INFERENCE: this is a trust/expertise signal that Bella Mia's indexed pages don't show.
10. **Clear package ladder.** Full Service Planning & Design / Partial Planning & Design / Wedding Management, and a questionnaire that captures date, location, budget and theme before a call. INFERENCE: it qualifies leads before Founder time is spent.

### Serene's weaknesses (so Bella Mia knows where it can beat them)

- Core page titles are placeholder-grade ("HOME | Serene Events"). The homepage does not say "wedding planner" or a city in its title. HIGH IMPACT for them.
- Two different title suffixes ("| Serene Events" on pages vs "| sereneeventsanddesign.com" on posts). LOW, but it signals nobody has configured the SEO plugin.
- Indexed tag archives ("design", "sandiegowedding") are thin and dilute crawl. MEDIUM.
- Did not appear organically for "wedding planner San Diego" or "Encinitas wedding planner" in this observation. Their own site is not carrying the head terms; directories and Facebook are.
- No pricing anywhere observed. Same as Bella Mia; neutral.
- Baltimore post and tag introduce geographic noise. LOW.

## 6. Priorities for Bella Mia

**DO NOW** (Founder, low effort, high exposure)
1. Fix the homepage, About and Contact title tags and meta descriptions (B1, B4). Effort: under an hour once wording is approved.
2. Canonicalize one host and redirect the other (B2). Effort: platform setting.
3. Resolve the `untitled-c1izt` page (B3). Effort: 30 minutes after reading it.
4. Pick one business location/service-area story and align WeddingWire, Knot, Yelp, PartySlate, Nextdoor, BBB, Facebook (B6). Effort: 2–3 hours.
5. Capture a baseline: Search Console (clicks, impressions, queries, indexed pages, 16 months), Business Profile interactions, form/call/"how did you hear about us" counts (section 7).

**NEXT** (4–8 weeks)
6. Launch a Real Weddings section. Target: 4–6 venue-named posts from VERIFIED past weddings, each with venue, city, what Bella Mia did (planning and/or florals), 300+ words of specific detail, real photos with the photographer credited. Start with Coronado/Loews only if Concierge confirms the relationship language.
7. One Coronado page only if Bella Mia genuinely works there regularly and it can hold substantive, true, Coronado-specific content (venues, logistics, what Bella Mia knows about them). It must pass the location-page test; otherwise skip.
8. Put a "Florals" section or page in place with real work, because "wedding design and florals San Diego" has dedicated competitors.

**LATER**
9. A San Diego venue guide once there are enough real-wedding posts to link to.
10. LocalBusiness / Organization schema after the true business details are settled.

**STOP / DO NOT**
- Do not target "Chula Vista wedding planner" if the positioning is luxury/hotel; the SERP is budget-signalled.
- Do not create city pages for every San Diego neighborhood.
- Do not add hotel or "preferred vendor" language to titles, pages or schema until VERIFIED and signed off by Concierge.
- Do not chase Serene's tag-archive pattern; it is a byproduct, not a strategy.
